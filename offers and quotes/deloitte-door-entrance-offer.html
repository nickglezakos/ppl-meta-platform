Executive Summary

EyeNet is an on-premises, edge-computing computer vision platform for human activity monitoring and intelligent automation. It transforms standard cameras into powerful machine vision nodes — tracking and regulating human activity, empowering digital signage with real-time triggers, and taking complete control of entrance and exit points.

A complete, on-premises door entrance solution combining real-time people detection, staff identification, automated alerts, digital signage, and full analytics — delivered through nine modularly licensed EyeNet products.

This offer addresses a single entrance door at Deloitte's offices, equipping it with intelligent monitoring that can distinguish staff from visitors in real time, trigger automated alerts and signage content changes, and provide comprehensive entry analytics.

The solution is built on a two-device architecture: an industrial fanless mini PC (32 GB RAM) running the full EyeNet backend platform in a secure location, and a compact, fanless Raspberry Pi Compute Module 5 edge unit at the door handling camera input, presence verification, and digital signage display on an integrated touch screen.

Two cameras serve complementary roles: an RTSP IP camera (TP-Link Tapo) for continuous instant detection — counting people, reading demographics, and matching against staff groups — and a Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 Wide dedicated to QR-based and face-detection Presence check-in flows. Eyenet Vradar is the camera-based instant detection engine that powers both: it captures people count, demographics, and face data in real time and can be assigned to any trigger-action loop in Eyenet Logic.

Commercial objective

Deliver a complete, production-ready door entrance solution under a single software environment with nine licensed EyeNet products, enabling Deloitte to monitor, identify, alert, display, and analyze all door activity from day one — with a clear path to expand to additional doors or sites under the same license structure. The platform infrastructure and secure VPN connectivity are covered by Eyenet Matrix at no additional charge.

The Door Entrance Use Case

A unified platform covering entry verification, staff identification, real-time alerts, analytics, and adaptive door-screen content — all driven by live camera detection and automation rules.

How It Works

  1. Detection (Eyenet Vradar & Eyenet Gate) — The Tapo IP camera, powered by Eyenet Vradar, continuously monitors the door area. Vradar is EyeNet's camera-based instant detection engine: it captures people count, demographics, and face data in real time and is governed by Eyenet Gate which manages up to 5 cameras per installation. Vradar can be assigned to any trigger-action loop in Eyenet Logic.
  2. Identification (Eyenet VProfiles) — Detected faces are matched against Deloitte's registered staff profiles via the vmeta service. Eyenet VProfiles organizes staff members as individual identity profile objects grouped into collections — each staff member is a VProfile within a VProfile group. The system instantly determines: is this a known staff member or an unknown visitor?
  3. Automation (Eyenet Logic) — Eyenet Logic is the event-driven trigger and action automation engine. It connects detection events from Vradar to concrete actions. Triggers fire based on the match outcome:
    • Staff member detected → switch door signage to internal announcements, log the entry
    • Unknown person detected → alert security via email/Slack, switch signage to visitor instructions, log for audit
    • VIP staff matched → notify executive assistant, show premium welcome screen
  4. Presence Verification (Eyenet Presence) — Staff can also check in via QR (scanning the station QR on the touch screen or presenting their mobile QR) with optional face-detection confirmation for two-factor assurance. Eyenet Presence provides the authoritative log of every person passing through the door, with session traceability, policy-driven grant/deny decisions, and built-in reporting.
  5. Analytics (Eyenet Insights & Eyenet Matrix) — Every entry is logged with session traces: who entered, when, via which method, and under which policy. Eyenet Insights dashboards show staff vs visitor ratios, peak hours, demographics, and trigger performance. Eyenet Matrix enables cross-site aggregation for future multi-site expansion and covers the platform infrastructure and secure VPN connectivity.
