Executive Summary

EyeNet Inspect is a fully developed, production-ready industrial vision module within the EyeNet platform that automates surface defect detection on continuous production lines — detecting cracks, scratches, pits, and markings in real time with sub-5 ms inline response.

A complete, on-premises industrial inspection system combining four synchronized high-resolution cameras, an industrial edge compute unit, and AI-powered defect detection — all running on EyeNet's proven edge-computing architecture with zero cloud dependency.

This project outline addresses a single copper tube line running as part of a continuous production line, equipping it with full 360° circumferential surface inspection. Four 5-megapixel global-shutter GigE Vision cameras are arranged around the tube on a rigid ring bracket, each covering a 90°–120° arc of the surface. A co-located industrial edge PC runs real-time inference, emitting a hardware pass/fail signal within milliseconds of detecting a defect — fast enough to stop a conveyor or activate a marking actuator.

The system inspects a 10–20 cm axial segment of tube moving at up to 7 cm/sec, capturing over 5,000 frames per tube across all four cameras. Defect records — including cropped images, bounding boxes, classification, confidence scores, and position data — are forwarded to the EyeNet Node for dashboard display, alerting, and archival.

EyeNet Inspect is the sixth application module in the EyeNet platform, joining Presence, Signage, Vradar, Gate, and Sentinel. It leverages the same microservice architecture, camera management, trigger/action pipeline, and frontend dashboard framework — purpose-built for manufacturing quality assurance on high-speed production lines.

Commercial objective

Deliver a complete, production-ready copper tube surface inspection system under a single software environment, enabling the customer to detect, classify, log, and respond to surface defects in real time — with a clear path to expand to additional inspection lines or product types under the same license structure.

The Copper Tube Inspection Use Case

A unified inspection platform covering full 360° surface monitoring, real-time defect detection, automated inline response, and comprehensive quality analytics — all driven by live camera inference and configurable automation rules.

How It Works

  1. 360° Image Capture — Four synchronized 5 MP global-shutter cameras, arranged around the tube on a rigid ring at a single inspection station, capture the full circumference simultaneously. Hardware GPIO triggering at 30 Hz ensures all cameras fire at the exact same instant — no spatial offset between frames. Each camera views a 10–20 cm axial segment of the tube with 90°–120° circumferential coverage.
  2. Real-Time AI Defect Detection — A trained defect detection model running on the co-located industrial edge PC analyzes every frame as it arrives. The model identifies and classifies surface defects — cracks, scratches, pits, and surface markings — with bounding box localization and confidence scoring. Inference completes in under 7 milliseconds per camera, well within the 33.3 ms frame budget at 30 fps.
  3. Inline Pass/Fail Response — When a critical defect is detected, the edge PC emits a hardware GPIO signal within 5 milliseconds — fast enough to stop the conveyor, activate a marking spray nozzle, or trigger an air ejector. Simultaneously, defect metadata (class, confidence, bounding box, position, timestamp) plus a cropped image of the defect are forwarded to the EyeNet Node over the local network.
  4. Quality Dashboard & Analytics — Every defect is logged with full traceability: which camera detected it, at what position along the tube, with what confidence, and under which model version. The EyeNet dashboard displays a live multi-camera tile view with defect overlays, a defect gallery for review, trend charts showing defect rates over time, and per-batch quality reports.
  5. Automated Alerts & Reporting — Configurable triggers fire on defect events: Slack or Teams notifications for critical defects, automatic batch quality reports at the end of each production run, and escalation if defect rates exceed defined thresholds.

Value Summary

Operational Benefits

  • 100% inline inspection — Every millimeter of every tube is inspected across the full 360° circumference. No sampling, no off-line QA bottlenecks.
  • Real-time rejection — Hardware GPIO signal within 5 ms of defect detection stops the conveyor or activates a marker before the defect leaves the inspection station.
  • Consistent, objective quality — AI-driven defect classification eliminates operator subjectivity and fatigue. Same standard applied to every tube, every shift.
  • Full traceability — Every defect is logged with image, class, confidence, position, and timestamp. Batch quality reports provide auditable quality records for customers and compliance.
  • On-premises, air-gapped capable — All processing happens locally on the edge PC. No cloud dependency for video or data. Internet needed only for optional remote notifications.
  • Hardware flexibility — Works with standard industrial GigE Vision cameras from Basler, FLIR, Allied Vision, or IDS. No proprietary camera lock-in.

Commercial Benefits

  • Modular licensing — Start with one inspection line. Expand to additional lines, product types, or defect classes under the same license structure.
  • Single platform — EyeNet Inspect runs on the same EyeNet platform as Presence, Signage, Vradar, Gate, and Sentinel. One software environment, one commercial relationship.
  • Rapid deployment — The inspection module is already developed and production-ready. Deployment focuses on camera mounting, lighting setup, and model training for the specific product — not building software from scratch.
  • Integration-ready — REST API and webhook connectors enable integration with existing MES, SCADA, ERP, and quality management systems.
  • Clear expansion path — Add inspection lines, add defect classes, retrain models for new products, or aggregate quality data across multiple factories via EyeNet Matrix.

Assumptions and Exclusions

  • The customer provides the production line infrastructure (conveyor, tube handling, mounting points for the camera ring bracket), local networking equipment (Gigabit Ethernet switch), Ethernet cabling, and any mechanical or civil works required for installation.
  • The hardware items listed in this project outline (cameras, lenses, edge PC, lighting, trigger generator, cabling) are indicative recommendations. The customer may procure equivalent or alternative hardware; the software license and integration services are independent of hardware procurement.
  • Model training — see Deployment Service inclusion below.
Deployment Service — Included Model Training: Model training for the specific copper tube product is included as part of the deployment service. This requires a data collection phase: running 50–100 tubes through the inspection station to collect good samples, plus 20–30 tubes with artificially introduced defects for defect samples. Data labeling and model training are performed by EyeNet's engineering team. Defect class definitions (what constitutes a "crack" vs a "scratch" for this specific product) are agreed with the customer's quality team during this phase.
  • On-site installation, camera ring bracket fabrication, cable routing, and custom software development are excluded unless separately quoted.
  • The EyeNet Node (platform mini PC running the backend services) is assumed to be on the same local network as the edge PC. Remote management is provided through the included EyeNet VPN Mesh.
  • Taxes such as VAT are not included and will be applied according to applicable regulations.
