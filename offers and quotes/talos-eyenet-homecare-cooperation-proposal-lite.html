Executive Summary

A strategic technology cooperation where TMAIX's eHealth ecosystem and EyeNet's computer-vision platform jointly deliver an operational home care solution — keeping care staff on schedule, securing each patient's home, and detecting falls and calls for help in real time.

This integration is delivered through TMAIX's ePpocrates eCloud application solution, with EyeNet operating in headless mode as the intelligent bridge between the two platforms. It hosts connectors embedding home care business logic across both companies' systems, exposes fully tokenized (secured) endpoints, and publishes every capability as an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server — one per service — so AI agents, clinicians, and care teams can consume integrated vision + health workflows securely and uniformly.

TMAIX — on the one side, delivers the certified eHealth ecosystem and the ePpocrates eCloud application solution for home care.

Background

2.1  TMAIX

TMAIX is a premier provider of certified eHealth technologies (FDA-approved, CE-certified, ISO 9001/13485/27001), serving governments, hospitals, clinics, and home care providers. All capabilities in this cooperation are delivered through its ePpocrates eCloud application solution:

ePpocrates eCloud Application

Secure home care platform for scheduling, personal health records, and 24/7 patient monitoring — the single gateway to all integrated capabilities.

Telemedicine

Remote consultation and continuous clinical coordination for home care teams.

EMR / HIS

Clinical records and visit workflows that keep home-visit scheduling and documentation in order.

IoMT / eHealth

Medical sensors and wearables feeding real-time patient vitals and activity into the platform.

2.2  EyeNet

EyeNet is an on-premises, edge-computing computer-vision platform for human activity monitoring and intelligent automation, deployed at the patient's home. Characteristics relevant to this partnership:

  • Presence Module — confirms caregivers are in their positions where and when they should be during home visits.
  • Secure Zones — defines and monitors safe areas in and around the patient's home.
  • Automation Module — raises alarms for unspecified persons and triggers fall detection (already developed).
  • Voice / Natural Language — camera microphones parse phonetic commands and natural speech to trigger help requests (already developed).
  • Headless Mode — all functionality exposed programmatically with zero cloud dependency for video or data.

2.3  The Opportunity

Home care staff work across many different homes every day, and each patient's home is an unmonitored, unsecured environment. Today the two systems operate in silos: TMAIX's ePpocrates eCloud cares for people at home; EyeNet sees what happens in each home. Integrating them ensures caregivers are where they should be, turns every patient's home into a monitored and secured zone, and turns falls and calls for help into immediate, actionable triggers — all served through a single application.

3.1  Concept

3.2  Core Components

TMAIX REST API Connectors

Wrap TMAIX services (ePpocrates eCloud schedule, EMR/HIS records, IoMT vitals feeds, telemedicine session management) behind uniform adapter interfaces, mapping TMAIX data models (patients, visits, devices) to integration-domain objects.

EyeNet Headless Connectors

Consume EyeNet events and data streams — caregiver presence, secure-zone state, fall triggers, voice / help-request events — and abstract EyeNet's internal APIs behind stable contracts so version upgrades remain transparent to TMAIX.

Connector Logic & Orchestration Engine

Configurable home care business rules spanning both systems, with an event-driven core (publish/subscribe) plus synchronous request/response paths.

Integration Dashboard & Connectors Framework

An administration & operations dashboard visualizes and manages every connector. Each connector embeds its own trigger-&-action logic, modeled on the EyeNet platform, keeping system-to-system interoperability fully decoupled from the MCP servers.

Tokenization & Security Layer

Every endpoint tokenized — no direct system-to-system calls; OAuth 2.0 / JWT scoped tokens with short TTLs, per-client/per-service issuance, full data-flow audit trail aligned with TMAIX's ISO 27001 posture, and fully offline/on-premises operation.

MCP Server per Service

Each service is published as its own MCP server in a standardized, agent-ready interface. Examples: mcp-care-presence, mcp-secure-zone, mcp-fall-detection, mcp-voice-trigger, mcp-care-visit.

Illustrative Use Case · Home Care

The cooperation is scoped to a single, well-defined use case: safe and reliable home care delivery.

4.1  Staff Presence Assurance

a)  The EyeNet presence module contributes to making sure that the medical, paramedical, and support staff that conduct the home care services are in their positions where and when they should be — confirming that every scheduled home visit is delivered by the right caregiver, on time and in place.

4.2  Secure Zones at the Patient's Home

b)  EyeNet establishes secure zones in and around the patient's home, within which:

  • b1 · Unspecified-person alarm. There are alarms if any unspecified person is spotted, using the EyeNet automation module.
  • b2 · Fall-detection trigger. Within these zones EyeNet produces a fall-detection trigger via automation — an already-developed EyeNet module.
  • b3 · Voice & natural-language trigger. The EyeNet cameras use their microphones for phonetic commands and natural voice parsing, creating another crucial trigger whenever someone in the house calls for help or calls for urgent communication — an already-developed EyeNet module.
All of the above will be available via the ePpocrates eCloud application solution from TMAIX.
5

Joint ML Model Development

The cooperation develops dedicated machine-learning models trained on the combined data acquired by the sensors of both systems, delivering every insight to users through the ePpocrates eCloud application solution from TMAIX.

5.1  How the Models Are Developed

The models are built on the fusion of machine-vision output (presence, secure zones, movement, fall and voice triggers) with telemedicine & home-care sensory output (vitals from IoMT devices, patient monitoring streams, EMR/HIS and ePpocrates eCloud events, TMAIX REST events). They build directly on EyeNet's already-developed modules for fall detection and for voice / natural-language parsing, which are refined, validated, and continuously improved as they are used across the many homes served by each care team. Every model is productized behind the documented, tokenized interfaces of the integration layer and delivered to users through the ePpocrates eCloud application.

5.2  What the Models Deliver in the Home

Presence assurance — confirms the right caregiver is in the right home at the right time, every visit.

Fall & voice triggers — turns a detected fall, or a spoken call for help, into an immediate, reliable alert.

Secure-zone intelligence — recognizes who belongs in the home and raises alarms for unspecified persons.

5.3  Benefits for All Stakeholders — from the Users' Perspective

For the patient & family

  • Peace of mind knowing every home is monitored and secured around the clock.
  • A fall or a call for help is never missed — help is triggered automatically.
  • Familiar caregivers arrive where and when they should, without surprise.

For care staff (medical, paramedical, support)

  • Clear confirmation that they are in position at the right home and time.
  • Immediate, actionable alerts instead of manual monitoring and guesswork.
  • Simpler, safer visits in homes that are monitored and secured.

For care providers & organisations

  • Assured quality of care with verifiable staff presence at every visit.
  • Fewer missed or delayed visits, and faster escalation of urgent situations.
  • One operational picture across all homes, viewed in a single application.

For everyone — one single application

  • Every alert, confirmation and health insight is delivered through the ePpocrates eCloud application.
  • No extra tools or technical effort — the models work quietly behind the scenes.
  • Continuous improvement: the more the models are used, the more reliable they become.

5.4  Data Governance

All model development operates under strict data-governance rules consistent with GDPR, medical-device regulations, and both parties' certifications — anonymization/pseudonymization at the integration boundary, on-premises processing aligned with EyeNet's zero-cloud principle, and explicit consent/role-based access for any patient-related data.
