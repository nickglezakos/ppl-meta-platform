Homecare Presentation — Image Prompts

Professional & minimal photo prompts to illustrate the use cases on slides 11 & 12 of the TMA Homecare Presentation, for use with AI image generators (Midjourney / DALL·E / Stable Diffusion / Firefly).

Universal style block — append to every prompt

minimalist professional healthcare photography, soft natural daylight, clean uncluttered composition, muted neutral palette with soft whites greys and a single subtle navy-blue accent, shallow depth of field, realistic photography, high resolution, editorial style, no text, no words, no logos, no watermarks

Use a consistent aspect ratio per placement: 16:9 (wide hero / title band), 4:5 or 1:1 (feature cards & grid), 3:4 (portrait accents).
