Capital Requirements & Funding Strategy

Funding Logic: To minimize dilution and align capital with key milestones, the funding strategy is staged: an initial seed round of $500,000 covers the first year of operations, product development, and launch. A follow-on Series A/growth round is planned after demonstrating traction, supporting scale-up and market expansion at a higher valuation.

Staged Funding Plan: $500K Seed + Series A Growth

Allocation:

  • Seed Round (Year 1): $500K for product development, initial team, launch, and working capital
  • Series A / Growth (Year 2+): Additional capital (est. $700K+) for market expansion, sales, and scaling operations, raised after key milestones are achieved

Use of Seed Funds:

  • Personnel & Payroll: $180K
  • Marketing & CAC: $135K
  • Technology & Infrastructure: $125K
  • Operations, R&D, and Contingency: $60K

Note: This staged approach reduces early dilution, matches capital to risk, and enables a stronger Series A raise after demonstrating product-market fit and initial revenue traction.
