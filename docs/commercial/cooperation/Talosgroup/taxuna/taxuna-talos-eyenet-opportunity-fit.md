## 2. Lens B — Talos products only ([talosgroup.eu](https://talosgroup.eu/))

> Same opportunity order. Fit judged only against the Talos / TMA catalogue — no EyeNet layer.

1. **Medicines-shortage discovery (DHSC)** — No product applies. The catalogue watches the patient and the clinical record; this brief is about the medicines supply chain.  
   *Fit: No product*

2. **Electronic prescribing (Sheffield · PN426)** — Thin fit through **eWAVE MD** only — the hospital clinical system that sits beside prescribing. Nothing in the catalogue is an electronic prescribing product.  
   *Fit: Thin fit · eWAVE MD*

3. **Cancer referral pathways (St Vincent’s, Ireland)** — Thin fit through **eWAVE MD** and the analytics layer — the hospital record and workflow a referral pathway would run on. Nothing in the catalogue is a cancer-referral product.  
   *Fit: Thin fit · eWAVE MD · Analytics*

4. **AI Dynamic Purchasing System (RM6200)** — A route for AI already in the catalogue: **No.A.H.**, **Epione**, and deterioration analytics. No care problem is defined.  
   *Fit: Route only · No.A.H. · Epione*

5. **Spark DPS (RM6094)** — A route for an early pilot of **Epione**, **MediStation**, or **No.A.H.** No care problem is defined.  
   *Fit: Route only · Epione · MediStation*

6. **Extra Due North link** — No product can be matched. The brief has a link and no description.  
   *Fit: Unknown*

7. **NHS Innovation Service** — A door for **ePokratis**, the IoMT monitoring kit, **MediStation**, and **Epione** to be introduced to the NHS — introduction route, not a purchase.  
   *Fit: Introduction route · ePokratis*

8. **HSE Virtual Ward DPS** — The direct fit. **ePokratis** plus IoMT vitals, telemedicine, and the clinical record is the remote-monitoring core of hospital-level care at home.  
   *Fit: Direct fit · ePokratis · IoMT · Telemedicine*

> Under both lenses, **HSE Virtual Ward** is the only direct problem–product match. Everything else is either outside scope, a thin hospital-system adjacency, an unknown link, or a buying / introduction route with no defined care problem.
