What HSE is buying

The HSE seeks a market-proven, end-to-end vendor-managed Remote Health Monitoring solution to support Virtual Wards: time-limited, hospital-level care at home as an alternative to ED admission and/or to enable earlier discharge — for adult, older persons, and paediatric services across respiratory, cardiology, endocrinology and related pathways.

TMAIX proposes a jointly delivered operational solution built on its certified eHealth / IoMT stack (ePpocrates / ePokratis eCloud + MedAiConnect) and EyeNet’s existing on-premises computer-vision modules for fall, voice help-call, presence, and secure-zone safety in the unstaffed home. Clinical responsibility and governance remain with the HSE multidisciplinary team (MDT).

Eight selling points

1One vendor-managed virtual ward at home

TMAIX is the single accountable supplier: clinical application, measurement equipment, connectivity, training, and ongoing support. EyeNet is embedded as the in-home safety layer — not a second product the hospital must buy or integrate separately.

2Market-ready remote monitoring core

Certified eHealth stack (FDA / CE / ISO 9001 / 13485 / 27001 as stated in the cooperation materials), with PHR/EMR, telemedicine, and IoMT vitals already in product — SpO2, blood pressure, glucose/ketone, ECG, temperature, weight, spirometry — covering respiratory, cardiology, and endocrinology pathway kits.

3Web-based MDT channel

Clinicians see vitals trends, records, video consultation, and alerts in one application. Patients and families use the same channel from home. One operational picture for the Virtual Ward MDT — no tool sprawl.

4Hospital-grade safety in an unstaffed home

EyeNet’s already-developed fall detection, voice / help-call parsing, presence confirmation, and secure-zone alarms turn the patient’s home into a monitored care environment. This is the differentiator versus vitals-only RPM vendors — especially for older persons and high-acuity early discharge.

5Privacy by architecture

Video and vision processing stay on-premises / at the edge; only tokenized clinical events (fall, help, presence, zone) reach the TMAIX application. This supports GDPR-aligned, privacy-by-design deployment for Irish health data — without claiming a GDPR “certificate.”

6Fusion early-warning roadmap

Joint models can combine IoMT trends with mobility and activity signals for deterioration, fall-risk, and post-acute recovery insight. Positioned as a product roadmap on top of live modules — not as a finished Irish clinical trial claim.

7Governance and audit

OAuth 2.0 / JWT tokenization, scoped access, full audit trail, and role-based controls. Clinical responsibility stays with the HSE MDT; TMAIX operates the technology under processor / vendor-managed terms with clear lines of accountability.

8Immediate deployment, national scale later

Built on existing products, not a research build. Pathway packs can start with respiratory, cardiology, and endocrinology and extend to older persons and paediatrics. Training and education are included to support Health Region rollout and alignment with Sláintecare’s community-care direction.
