Why this solution

  • Works at sea with zero cloud dependency — presence, alerts, fall detection, and PPE colour rules run on the vessel; HQ syncs when connected to VPN or satellite.
  • Know who is onboard, where, and on watch — Presence, Gate, and Sentinel close the gap between ERP schedules and real attendance.
  • Spot a fallen seafarer in seconds — body detection on Vradar, not a missed round or late radio check.
  • Enforce protective clothing by colour — colour analytics alerts when clothing in a designated area is not close to the colours assigned in the trigger.
  • Three-tier notifications — onboard (instant), ship-to-HQ (VPN / satellite), ERP crew module (bi-directional).
  • Scale like an industrial site — one mini PC + 2–5 cameras per spot; full medium/large-ship coverage ≈ small/medium industrial site.
  • Fleet-wide Matrix — one HQ view across vessels, with ACL-isolated VPN mesh and no public exposure of video.

Scenario 01

Watch officer absent from the bridge

An unattended watch is caught by the system — not by the next incident.

  • Sentinel detects absence beyond a configured grace period (e.g. bridge wing vs cargo office thresholds).
  • Escalating chain: first to the OOW mobile, then captain and chief mate if unresolved.
  • Presence ties the post to the right person and schedule imported from the ERP crew module.
  • Attendance records export back to ERP — shore-side ops see verified watch compliance.
  • Fully local: zero internet required for detection and onboard alerting.

Scenario 02

PPE / protective clothing colour mismatch

If the colours are wrong for the zone, the trigger fires — before the work continues.

  • Colour analytics compare worn clothing to the protective colours assigned per designated area.
  • Alert when colours are not close to the trigger set — hi-vis, boiler-suit, deck PPE colour profiles.
  • Per-zone rules: engine room, cargo deck, enclosed work areas each carry their own colour set.
  • Stops “almost wearing PPE” ambiguity — enforcement is visual, continuous, and logged.
  • Compliance events export to ERP / HSEQ for fleet safety analysis.

Scenario 03

Fallen seafarer on deck / in machinery space

Spot the fall before the next round — response in seconds, not after a missed check.

  • Body detection recognises a collapsed or prone person on deck, in machinery spaces, cargo holds, and corridors.
  • Instant priority alert to security officer eyewear, duty officer, captain — and medical team when needed.
  • Zone + timestamp logged for HSEQ; no wait for radio silence or a late UMS round.
  • Works in Goggles, Area, and Drone modes — patrol, fixed spot, or open-deck aerial view.
  • Complements Sentinel lone-worker rules: fall trigger escalates even if movement thresholds have not yet expired.

Scenario 04

Crew member misses muster in an emergency

Live headcount at every muster station — who is accounted for, who is still missing.

  • Gate tracks movement toward muster; Presence counts people at each station in Area mode.
  • Captain and chief mate see a live picture — no manual roll call under stress.
  • Cross-check against the ERP crew manifest: scheduled crew vs people actually present.
  • Automatic muster report with timestamps for drills and real emergencies — audit-ready.
  • Works offline at sea; DPA at HQ receives the report when connected.

Scenario 05

Unauthorised person in the engine room

Restricted zones stay enforced 24/7 — even when no one is watching the screen.

  • Presence / Area mode defines the engine-room perimeter; intrusion raises an immediate alert.
  • Vradar on patrol verifies faces against the crew manifest — unrecognised or wrong-zone personnel flagged.
  • Optional certification check against ERP: right person, right credentials for the space.
  • Chief engineer and security officer notified with location — captain escalated as needed.
  • No cloud: recognition and zone rules run on the vessel’s local nodes.

Scenario 06

Stowaway detected in pre-departure search

ISPS search, accelerated — every face checked against crew and visitor manifests.

  • Security officer uses Vradar goggles to scan compartments; unknown faces alert with location stamp.
  • Gate complements with gangway identity and embarkation logging.
  • Visitor / POI watchlists can be loaded before port calls — INTERPOL or PFS notices included.
  • Alert chain reaches CSO at HQ and, when required, port authority coordination.
  • Dramatically shortens search time versus manual compartment walkdowns alone.

Scenario 08

Visitor not disembarked before departure

No silent leftover on board — every visitor’s exit is timed and checked.

  • Shore-leave and visitor status can sync with the ERP crew / visitor modules.

Scenario 09

Smoke detected in a cargo hold

See the smoke early — then every display and alarm moves to emergency mode.

  • Fire Detect (vision) can catch smoke / flame in holds and machinery spaces before some conventional detectors trip.
  • Orchestrator fires the cascade: alarms, Signage override to evacuation routes, crew-wide messaging.
  • Captain and fire team notified immediately; HQ ops when the link allows.
  • Incident package with timestamps; optional cargo-manifest cross-reference for response context.
  • Emergency content on Signage works offline — pre-loaded, vision-triggered.

  • Cross-reference permit-to-work / confined-space entry logs for context.
  • Designed for hazardous spaces where a missed radio call is too late.
