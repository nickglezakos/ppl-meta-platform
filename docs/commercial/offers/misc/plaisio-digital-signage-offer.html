Executive Summary

A single software license covering the EyeNet main platform and the Digital Signage module with full functionality, including automation, audience analytics, triggers, remote playback control, and centralized campaign management across the Plaisio store network.

EyeNet Digital Signage helps customers start quickly with ordinary hardware, operate campaigns centrally, and grow toward intelligent audience-aware signage without replacing their whole infrastructure.

EyeNet Digital Signage is a simple, flexible, software-driven signage solution for a multi-store retail environment. It helps businesses deliver the right content on the right screen while connecting signage operations with live audience intelligence and analytics.

For Plaisio, the offer is structured around one software license for the main EyeNet platform and the Digital Signage module with full functionality. This includes digital signage operations, audience sensing, rule-based automation, triggers, and traffic analytics in one commercial package.

Instead of forcing a closed hardware model, the platform can run on standard PCs, laptops, and servers, supporting practical deployment choices for store-by-store rollout. The result is a platform that is easy to explain, easy to pilot, and easy to expand.

Simplicity and Deployment Model

From a sales and rollout perspective, onboarding is intentionally simple and staged. The platform can run on standard computing hardware and work with multiple camera types, reducing infrastructure friction and allowing adoption around the real environment of each store.

  1. Prepare a platform device such as a PC, laptop, or server.
  2. Connect the required cameras and signage devices where applicable.
  3. Ensure the local environment operates on the same network for reliable discovery and service communication.
  4. Upload media, create playlists, and assign content to each store application.
  5. Start with manual playback and expand into triggers, instant detection, and analytics as desired.

Supported practical deployment options

  • Standard PCs, laptops, and servers.
  • USB cameras connected to PCs or laptops.
  • USB cameras connected to Raspberry Pi devices.
  • Wi-Fi or IP cameras exposing RTSP streams.
  • Android mobile device cameras through the Android application.

Remote access is recommended through VPN into the same environment, keeping the operational model clear and easy to support.
