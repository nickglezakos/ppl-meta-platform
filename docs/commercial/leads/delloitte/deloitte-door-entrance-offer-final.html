The Door Entrance Use Case

A unified platform covering early approach detection (staircase and parking), staff identification, welcome / warning door-screen content, Presence fallback, analytics, and automation — all driven by live Vradar detection and Logic rules across three wired cameras.

How It Works

  1. Detection (Eyenet Vradar & Eyenet Gate) — Three TP-Link VIGI cameras, powered by Eyenet Vradar, continuously monitor the staircase corridor, the area just outside the door, and the small hallway inside. Vradar captures people count, demographics, and face data in real time, can issue Presence certificates when a match is strong enough, and is governed by Eyenet Gate (up to 5 cameras per installation). Any Vradar stream can drive trigger-action loops in Eyenet Logic.
  2. Identification (Eyenet VProfiles) — Detected faces are matched against Deloitte's registered staff profiles via the vmeta service. Eyenet VProfiles organizes staff as individual identity profile objects in collections. The system determines in real time: known staff member or unknown visitor?
  3. Automation (Eyenet Logic) — Eyenet Logic is the configurable rule engine connecting Vradar events to actions. Administrators can add, change, or retire custom triggers and actions without a software upgrade — new automation scenarios are configuration, not a release cycle. Typical rules for this site:
    • Staff VProfile matched (staircase or outdoor approach) → switch door signage to a welcome video / internal announcements, log the approach
    • Unknown person detected approaching → switch signage to a warning / visitor video, optional alert via email/Slack/Teams, log for audit
    • Close-range match still inconclusive on the outdoor door camera → open the Presence station UI on the door touch screen for standard QR / verification procedures
    • VIP staff matched → notify executive assistant, show premium welcome content
    • Optional — under strict authentication and security — push selected EyeNet events to a Deloitte-owned webhook when Deloitte requests integration with internal systems
  4. Presence Verification (Eyenet Presence) — When instant VProfile detection fails or policy requires a verified session, staff and visitors use the door touch screen for standard Presence flows (station QR, mobile QR, optional confirmation). Eyenet Presence is the authoritative log of who passed through the door, with session traceability, policy-driven grant/deny, and reporting. Vradar Presence certificates can short-circuit the flow when live matching is reliable.
  5. Analytics (Eyenet Insights & Eyenet Matrix) — Every entry is logged with session traces. Eyenet Insights delivers full operational analytics: staff vs visitor ratios, peak hours, demographics, per-camera / per-zone traffic, Presence grant types and outcomes, and Logic trigger performance — filterable and exportable. Eyenet Matrix covers EyeNet’s own platform infrastructure and secure VPN (so the installation can stay decoupled from Deloitte IT) plus future multi-site aggregation.

Preparatory Phase — Two-Week On-Premises Pilot

Before production rollout, the EyeNet team conducts a two-week on-premises preparatory phase at Deloitte's site using hardware similar to the configuration in this offer — so camera angles, lighting (including the dark underground parking approach), signage behaviour, and Presence fallback can be validated in the real environment.

Objectives

  • Confirm VIGI C340 / C440 placement, PoE cabling, and RTSP stability on Deloitte's LAN.
  • Tune Vradar and VProfiles matching under site lighting — especially the outdoor approach corner.
  • Configure and dry-run Eyenet Logic rules: staff match → welcome video; unknown approach → warning video; inconclusive match → Presence station on the door touch screen.
  • Validate the door Linux mini PC + touch screen for Signage playback and Presence UI.
  • Enrol a sample staff cohort into VProfiles and exercise the full entry path end-to-end.

What EyeNet Provides

  • On-site / on-premises EyeNet engineering presence for up to two calendar weeks.
  • Temporary pilot stack equivalent in role to the offered platform mini PC, door Linux player, touch screen, and three VIGI-class PoE cameras (or identical models when available).
  • Configuration of Gate, Vradar, VProfiles, Logic, Presence, Signage, Insights, Matrix, and API for the pilot door.
  • A short findings report: recommended mounts, Logic rule set, and any site-specific adjustments before production hardware is ordered.

Pilot hardware remains EyeNet property unless otherwise agreed. Software licensing for the production phase follows Section 7; commercial terms for the preparatory phase can be agreed separately or bundled with optional remote onboarding.
