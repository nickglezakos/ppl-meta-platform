I need a background process that has its own endpoint and is applied per collection.
The background process is named tracking background process and it does the following:
a) executes the cross video tracking endpoint for specific tracking batches of videos in the collection 
and b)stores the results of the cross video tracking to history logs.

The tracking background process has the following functionalities:
1.
It can be activated and deactivated by the user. When activated it executes tracking batches in the background.
2.
Upon activation the user defines the timeframe of activation (start - end).
3.
It has a tracking batch value which is the number of videos it activates the cross video tracking
endpoint for. The tracking batch value has a default value and can also be modified via the user.
The default value is 3. That means that is executes the cross vodeo tracking endpoint for batches
of 3 videos each.
4.
To execute a batch, it first gets the start detatime and end datetime data of the batch
and then executes the cross video tracking endpoint. The start datetime of the batch is the start
datetime of the first video and the last datetime of the batch is the last datetime of the last video.
If there are videos left after sorting the entire activation span that are less than the tracking batch value 
it executes the tracking process on them as a single batch regardless.
5.
It stores the timeframes it has been succefully executed (history logs).
6.
It stores the results of the cross video tracking endpoint per batch.
7.
Upon execution the tracking background process checks its history logs for batches that have produced results whithin the timeframe of activation. 
If there are any such batch results stored it skips them.
