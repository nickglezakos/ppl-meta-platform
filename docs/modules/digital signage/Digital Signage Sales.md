# Digital Signage Sales

> **Purpose:** Short executive summary for sales conversations and high-level customer onboarding  
> **Module:** Digital Signage  
> **Platform:** PPL Meta

---

## 1. Executive Summary

PPL Meta Digital Signage is a simple, flexible, software-driven signage solution that helps businesses deliver the right content on the right screen while connecting signage operations with live audience intelligence.

It is designed to be easy to explain, easy to pilot, and easy to expand.

Instead of forcing customers into closed hardware ecosystems or expensive fixed-purpose kiosk systems, PPL Meta gives them a practical platform that can:

- run on standard computing hardware
- work with multiple camera types
- support multiple screen formats
- connect signage with triggers, instant detection, and analytics
- scale from one site or one zone to a broader rollout

For sales and onboarding conversations, the simplest message is:

**PPL Meta Digital Signage helps customers start quickly with ordinary hardware, operate campaigns centrally, and grow toward intelligent audience-aware signage without replacing their whole infrastructure.**

## 2. What The Customer Gets

At a high level, the Digital Signage module allows customers to:

- create playlists from uploaded video content
- assign content to signage devices
- manage screens from a central interface

- connect signage with live camera-based audience signals
- use analytics to understand who is present in each zone and how to optimize content

This makes the platform suitable for:

- retail stores
- showrooms
- hospitality environments
- counters and service desks
- entrance displays
- hero walls and campaign zones
- compact deployments using laptops or small edge devices

## 4. Hardware And Camera Flexibility

One of the strongest commercial advantages of the platform is its openness.

### Supported platform device types

PPL Meta can run on standard:

- PCs
- laptops
- servers

This allows customers to deploy the platform using existing or easy-to-source computing infrastructure instead of committing to a specialized appliance stack.

### Supported camera options

The platform is designed to work with many practical camera sources, including:

- USB cameras connected to PCs or laptops
- USB cameras connected to Raspberry Pi devices
- Wi-Fi / IP cameras that expose RTSP streams
- cameras such as the TP-Link Tapo series when RTSP is available
- Android mobile device cameras via the Android application

This is an important sales point because it reduces infrastructure friction and allows the customer to adopt the platform around their real environment.

### Supported signage endpoints

Signage playback can be used across different display styles, including:

- large displays
- entrance screens
- wall-mounted campaign screens
- counter screens
- tabletop laptop displays

## 5. Simplicity For Customer Onboarding

From a sales perspective, onboarding should be presented as simple and staged.

### High-level onboarding flow

1. Prepare a platform device such as a PC, laptop, or server.
2. Connect the required cameras and signage devices.
3. Ensure all devices and services are operating on the same local network.
4. Upload media content.
5. Create playlists in the Digital Signage module.
6. Discover signage devices and assign content.
7. Start with manual playback.
8. Add triggers, instant detection, and analytics later if the customer wants intelligent automation.

### Why this matters in a sales conversation

This makes the product easier to position because customers do not need to buy into the full advanced model on day one.

They can start with:

- one platform device
- one or two cameras
- one or two screens
- one simple playlist workflow

Then expand as confidence grows.

## 6. Network Requirement

A key deployment requirement should be explained clearly and simply.

### Local network requirement

The platform device, whether it is a PC, laptop, or server, should operate on the **same network** as the connected peripheral devices and services.

This includes:

- cameras
- signage devices
- discovery-related services
- other local platform services

This same-network requirement simplifies:

- device discovery
- media access
- playback control
- service-to-service communication
- reliable local operation

### Remote access requirement

For remote access, management, or support, the recommended approach is **VPN access** into that same environment.

This gives customers a simple operational model:

- local devices communicate over the local network
- remote administrators connect securely through VPN

This is a strong onboarding message because it keeps the operational story clear and avoids overcomplicating remote deployment discussions.
