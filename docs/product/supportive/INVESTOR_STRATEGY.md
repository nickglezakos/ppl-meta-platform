## Executive Summary

EyeNet Vision is a dual-model (B2B enterprise + B2C consumer) intelligent vision technology platform addressing $2-4 billion addressable market across security, marketing, compliance, and operations verticals. The company offers six specialized applications leveraging proprietary demographic detection, facial recognition, and behavioral analytics.

**Investment Highlights**:
- **Proven Technology**: Beta-ready products with 5 applications ready for Q2 2026 launch
- **Dual Revenue Model**: B2B enterprise ($6K-$150K contracts) + B2C consumer ($20-$300/month subscriptions)
- **3-Year Revenue Trajectory**: $1M (2026) → $8M (2027) → $26M (2028)
- **Geographic Focus**: North America (US, Canada) + EU pilot (Greece, Romania)
- **Clear Exit Path**: IPO or strategic acquisition at $300M-$1B valuation (2029-2030)

## Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Key Investment Risks

**1. Regulatory & Privacy Compliance**
- **Risk**: GDPR, CCPA, facial recognition bans limit market opportunity
- **Mitigation**: Modular design allows disabling demographic features; age-only detection reduces PII collection; on-premises deployment option

**2. Market Competition**
- **Risk**: Large players (Google, Amazon, Meta) enter with massive resources
- **Mitigation**: Vertical specialization (6 focused applications vs. horizontal platform); proprietary algorithms; strong customer lock-in

**3. Technology Execution**
- **Risk**: Security Officer Agent hardware delays (smart glasses development)
- **Mitigation**: Software-first approach; 5 other products ready for launch; partner with hardware manufacturers vs. build in-house

**4. Customer Concentration**
- **Risk**: Early-stage dependency on pilot customers (potential for high churn)
- **Mitigation**: Diversified customer base across verticals; B2B + B2C model; focus on land-and-expand with enterprise customers

**5. Market Adoption**
- **Risk**: B2C consumer adoption slower than projected
- **Mitigation**: Conservative penetration assumptions (0.5-2%); freemium funnel to convert free users; focus on B2B if B2C underperforms

**6. Geopolitical & Economic**
- **Risk**: Economic downturn reduces enterprise IT spending
- **Mitigation**: Compliance products (Room & Gate Protection, Underage Detector) are non-discretionary; security spending stable in downturns

## Key Performance Indicators to Monitor

### Financial KPIs
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Track vs. plan, segment by B2B/B2C
- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**: Primary growth metric
- **Gross Margin**: Target 65-70%, expanding to 70%+ by Series C
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: B2B <$5K, B2C <$30
- **CAC Payback Period**: B2B <6 months, B2C <3 months
- **Burn Rate**: Track monthly cash burn vs. runway
- **Rule of 40**: Growth rate + profit margin (target: 50+)

### Customer KPIs
- **Customer Count**: B2B enterprise accounts + B2C paid users
- **Average Contract Value (ACV)**: B2B target $15-20K
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: Target 110-120%
- **Churn Rate**: B2B <5%, B2C <10%
- **Customer Concentration**: No customer >15% of revenue
- **Pipeline Coverage**: 3-4x pipeline-to-quota ratio

### Growth & Efficiency KPIs
- **Sales Efficiency (Magic Number)**: >0.75 (dollars generated per sales/marketing dollar)
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: Target 3:1 or higher
- **Months to Close (Sales Cycle)**: B2B 6-9 months, trend downward
- **Win Rate**: Target 20-30% of qualified pipeline
- **Free-to-Paid Conversion**: B2C target 5-10%
