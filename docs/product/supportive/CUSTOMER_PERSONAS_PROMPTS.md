# Customer Personas — AI Image Generation Prompts

**Reference Document**: CUSTOMER_PERSONAS.md  
**Purpose**: Analytical prompts for generating realistic visual representations of each customer persona described in the Customer Personas document.

---

## General Style Guidance

All images should be photorealistic, professional, and suitable for a business plan or investor presentation. Lighting should feel natural and contextual to the environment. People should appear authentic, engaged, and representative of the role described. Avoid stock-photo aesthetics — favor candid, in-context compositions.

## Persona 1 — Warehouse Manager

### Primary Portrait

> A mid-40s operations manager standing on the floor of a large modern warehouse, wearing a high-visibility vest over business-casual clothing, holding a tablet. Behind them, tall shelving racks with palletized goods extend into the distance under industrial LED lighting. A forklift is visible in the mid-ground. The manager has a confident, attentive expression, reviewing real-time data on their tablet screen. Shot at eye level, shallow depth of field, natural warehouse lighting with overhead fluorescents. Photorealistic, corporate documentary style.

### In-Context Scene

> Wide-angle shot of a busy distribution center interior during a shift change. In the foreground, a warehouse manager gestures toward a wall-mounted monitor displaying a live attendance dashboard with green check marks and personnel headcount numbers. Workers in safety vests and hard hats move through the background near loading docks. Pallets and forklifts are visible. The atmosphere is industrious and organized. Warm industrial lighting, slight motion blur on background workers, photorealistic editorial photography style.

### Technology Interaction

> Close-up, over-the-shoulder view of a warehouse manager's hands holding a tablet that displays a security camera grid view of a warehouse facility. On the screen, colored zones overlay the camera feeds indicating restricted areas in red and safe zones in green. The manager's high-visibility vest sleeve is partially visible. Background is softly blurred warehouse environment. Clean, modern UI visible on screen. Photorealistic, technology-focused commercial photography.

## Usage Notes

- **Aspect Ratios**: For presentation slides use 16:9. For document inserts use 4:3 or 3:2. For social media or brochure use 1:1 or 9:16 vertical crops.
- **Consistency**: When generating a full set, specify a consistent color temperature and photographic style across all personas to maintain visual cohesion in the business plan.
- **Diversity**: Vary the age, ethnicity, and gender of the people depicted across personas to reflect a global customer base.
- **Branding**: If Eyenet-vision branding elements (logo, UI colors) are available, request that dashboard screens and UI overlays incorporate them for brand consistency.
