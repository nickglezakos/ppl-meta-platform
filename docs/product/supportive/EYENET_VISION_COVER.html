Core Functionalities & Features
Version 2.23.1 January 15, 2026 Confidential eyenet-vision.com
"Eyenet-vision puts the power of advanced computer vision to work for everyday people — whether you run a small shop, manage a building, or simply want a smarter, safer space."

Instead of passive cameras that only record, the platform understands what it sees and acts on it automatically:

  • Count visitors so you know your busiest hours
  • Alert you the moment something unusual happens
  • Switch digital signs to show the right message at the right time
  • Send notifications straight to your phone — all without storing a single frame in the cloud
  • Everything runs locally on your own hardware, so your privacy stays intact and the system keeps working even if the internet goes down
  • No per-camera licenses, no mandatory subscriptions, and no technical ceiling — it scales from a single camera at home to hundreds across a campus, adapting to the hardware you already have
"In short, Eyenet-vision turns ordinary cameras into an intelligent, always-on assistant that helps you make better decisions, respond faster, and operate more efficiently every single day."
