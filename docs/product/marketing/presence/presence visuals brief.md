# Presence Visuals Brief

## Purpose

This brief contains prompt-ready visual directions for creating marketing personas and scene concepts for PPL Meta Presence. The prompts are grounded in the implemented presence capabilities: QR-only check-in, camera-only verification, QR plus camera verification, owner QR flows, policy-driven automation, analytics, audit traceability, and secure enterprise deployment.

The goal is to generate visuals that feel credible, product-aware, and operationally realistic rather than generic futuristic attendance imagery.

## Creative Direction

- Show real operational environments such as offices, warehouses, healthcare spaces, education campuses, logistics hubs, and controlled-entry buildings.
- Make mobile-device and station-device interactions visible.
- Include QR scanning, camera-based verification, and dashboard review moments.
- Present personas as confident, modern, privacy-aware professionals.
- Keep the visual language clean, enterprise-grade, and trustworthy.
- Avoid sci-fi holograms, exaggerated biometric effects, and surveillance-heavy aesthetics.
- Prefer realistic lighting, believable devices, and natural body language.

## Visual System Guidance

- Style: premium SaaS marketing photography or high-end product illustration.
- Tone: secure, efficient, human-centered, explainable.
- Composition: one clear hero persona and one visible presence action per scene.
- Devices: mobile phones, wall-mounted tablets, front desk displays, access gates, office cameras, dashboard screens.
- UI hints: QR codes, check-in confirmation, verification status, analytics dashboards, audit trace timelines.
- Privacy cues: subtle trust markers such as consent screens, secure environments, professional posture, non-invasive framing.

## Master Prompt Template

Use this structure when creating additional persona prompts:

"Create a high-end marketing visual for PPL Meta Presence showing [persona] in [environment] using [presence functionality]. Show [device interaction] and [supporting system cue]. The scene should communicate [business value] with a clean, enterprise SaaS aesthetic, realistic lighting, believable hardware, subtle UI overlays, and a professional, human-centered tone. Avoid futuristic holograms, cyberpunk styling, and invasive surveillance imagery."

## Persona Prompts

### 1. Front Desk Workplace Check-In

"Create a premium SaaS marketing image of a front desk coordinator in a modern corporate lobby using PPL Meta Presence for QR-based employee check-in. Show an employee scanning a station-side QR code with a mobile phone while a reception tablet displays a live presence confirmation state. Include subtle UI hints for check-in success, secure identity verification, and a professional office environment. The image should feel efficient, trustworthy, and enterprise-ready, with realistic lighting and polished contemporary interior design. Avoid sci-fi effects and generic stock-photo smiles."

### 2. Secure Office Verified Presence

"Create a realistic enterprise visual of an office employee completing a verified presence flow using mobile QR plus camera verification. Show the employee presenting a mobile QR to a station device and then standing naturally in front of a workspace camera for final confirmation. Include subtle product UI overlays for verified presence, decision status, and access granted. The environment should be a modern secure workplace with a calm, privacy-respectful feel. Avoid surveillance-heavy imagery and exaggerated face-recognition graphics."

### 3. Warehouse Shift Start Persona

"Create a high-quality marketing visual of a warehouse supervisor using PPL Meta Presence at shift start. Show workers checking in through a rugged station with QR scanning and camera-assisted validation. Include industrial but clean surroundings, safety gear, mobile devices, and a dashboard screen indicating successful presence verification for multiple staff members. The scene should emphasize speed, reliability, and operational control. Avoid gritty dystopian warehouse imagery."

### 4. Healthcare Staff Arrival Persona

"Create a polished healthcare operations visual showing a clinician arriving for a shift and using PPL Meta Presence for secure attendance verification. Show a hospital or clinic station where the user scans a QR code and completes a respectful, non-invasive camera-based verification step. Include subtle confirmation UI, clean medical surroundings, and a sense of compliance, trust, and efficiency. Avoid dramatic emergency-room chaos and invasive biometric visual effects."

### 5. Education Campus Access Persona

"Create a modern campus operations visual featuring a university staff member or student worker using PPL Meta Presence to verify attendance at a building entrance. Show a station-side QR code, a mobile phone interaction, and a campus access point with a light-touch verification workflow. Include subtle UI overlays for check-in status, timing, and secure entry. The overall mood should be organized, safe, and approachable. Avoid futuristic campus imagery or heavy security drama."

### 6. Retail Store Opening Persona

"Create a refined retail operations scene where a store manager uses PPL Meta Presence to confirm team arrival before opening. Show a back-office or staff entrance setting with a mobile QR interaction, a mounted device showing live session progress, and a simple dashboard indicating verified presence for the opening team. The visual should communicate punctuality, accountability, and smooth store operations. Avoid cluttered retail chaos and exaggerated technology effects."

### 7. Approved Owner QR Persona

"Create a product-aware marketing visual of an approved owner using PPL Meta Presence owner identity QR functionality. Show a professional user presenting an owner QR on a mobile screen to a station or checkpoint device, with subtle UI cues indicating trusted identity resolution and secure approval flow. The environment should feel executive, controlled, and enterprise-grade. Emphasize trust, permission, and system clarity rather than surveillance or enforcement."

### 8. Operations Manager Analytics Persona

"Create a premium enterprise dashboard scene featuring an operations manager reviewing PPL Meta Presence analytics. Show a professional manager in front of a large screen or laptop displaying user-by-day attendance summaries, grant types, outcomes, installation metrics, and session trace insights. The environment should be a clean operations office or command desk. The mood should communicate visibility, explainability, and actionable reporting. Avoid generic abstract charts detached from real use."

### 9. Security Administrator Audit Persona

"Create a realistic B2B marketing image of a security administrator reviewing a PPL Meta Presence session trace and audit trail. Show the administrator at a workstation with a clear but subtle interface displaying decision history, policy source, action execution details, and verification status. The setting should feel secure and professional, with restrained lighting and a strong sense of accountability and traceability. Avoid hacker aesthetics, dark neon themes, and alarmist visuals."

### 10. Multi-Site Enterprise Deployment Persona

"Create an enterprise marketing visual showing a facilities or IT leader managing PPL Meta Presence across multiple installations. Show a clean dashboard view with site-level metrics, presence status summaries, policy-driven workflows, and a sense of coordinated operations across several physical locations. Include one human operator and one secondary screen or wall display suggesting multi-site oversight. The image should communicate scale, governance, and operational consistency. Avoid overly futuristic control-room cliches."

### 11. Privacy-First Employee Persona

"Create a human-centered marketing visual for PPL Meta Presence focused on privacy and trust. Show an employee using a presence verification workflow with calm confidence, while subtle UI cues suggest secure handling, consent, and GDPR-aware operation. The environment should be a modern workplace with warm, natural lighting and minimal visual noise. The scene should feel respectful, transparent, and technologically mature. Avoid creepy biometric visuals or anything that implies loss of user control."

### 12. Fast Check-In Mobile Persona

"Create a crisp product marketing visual of a mobile-first professional using PPL Meta Presence for fast QR-only check-in. Show the user scanning a station QR with a smartphone and receiving an immediate check-in confirmation. Include subtle UI hints for speed, success, and clean interaction design. The setting should feel busy but organized, such as an office entrance or co-working reception. Emphasize ease of use and low friction. Avoid generic app mockup aesthetics detached from real space."

## Persona Variations To Request

When iterating on any prompt, vary along these dimensions:

- Industry: corporate, industrial, healthcare, education, retail, logistics.
- Role: employee, receptionist, manager, security admin, facilities lead, site operator.
- Functionality: QR only, camera only, QR plus camera, owner QR, reporting, session trace, policy controls.
- Device setup: kiosk, wall tablet, desktop console, laptop dashboard, handheld mobile, gate terminal.
- Output style: photorealistic, polished product illustration, editorial SaaS hero image.

## Negative Prompt Guidance

Use these exclusions when working with image-generation tools:

- No cyberpunk style.
- No sci-fi holograms.
- No glowing biometric mesh overlays on faces.
- No law-enforcement or interrogation mood.
- No dystopian surveillance room.
- No cluttered UI covering the entire image.
- No unrealistic hardware or impossible screen layouts.
- No exaggerated emotional expressions.
