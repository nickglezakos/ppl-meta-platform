## Slide 2 - Open Hardware and No Multi-Sensor Setup Burden

Create a 16:9 hero visual for a technology retail pitch deck slide titled Open Hardware and No Multi-Sensor Setup Burden.
Show a representative persona: a store IT and operations manager in their early 30s, confident and calm, setting up digital signage quickly in a modern electronics store.
The scene should clearly communicate simplicity and speed: one laptop, one standard camera, and one wall display are connected and already running campaign content.
Avoid any complex sensor rig, cables chaos, or proprietary hardware racks.
Include subtle visual storytelling that this used to be hard but is now easy: a small faded background hint of old bulky equipment, while the foreground is clean and streamlined.
Add light UI overlays suggesting Plug and Play Setup, Live in Minutes, and Open Hardware Compatible.
Style: premium, realistic, cinematic corporate photo-illustration, clean composition, natural human expression, professional lighting.
Color direction should match a modern retail tech brand palette: indigo, cyan, amber accents on neutral light background.
Leave clean negative space on the right side for slide text overlay.
No logos, no readable brand names, no watermark, no clutter.

Optional negative prompt:
low resolution, cartoonish, over-stylized sci-fi, messy cables, crowded background, distorted hands, extra limbs, text artifacts, logos, watermark, dark gloomy mood
