## Customer Personas & Use Cases

### System Requirements
For typical installations, Eyenet-vision operates efficiently on standard business hardware:
- **Minimum Hardware**: 16GB RAM, modern multi-core CPU (Intel i5/i7 or AMD equivalent)
- **Operating System**: Windows, Linux, or macOS
- **Storage**: Varies by camera count and retention period (typically 500GB - 2TB)
- **Network**: Standard Gigabit Ethernet (camera dependent)

This modest hardware requirement means most businesses can deploy Eyenet-vision without specialized server infrastructure.

## Implementation Pathways

### Start Small, Scale Up
Most customers begin with:
1. **Pilot Phase**: 2-4 cameras covering critical areas
2. **Proof of Value**: 30-90 days demonstrating ROI
3. **Expansion**: Additional cameras and advanced features
4. **Integration**: Connect with existing business systems

### Hardware Scaling Guide
- **1-10 cameras**: Single workstation (16GB RAM, quad-core CPU)
- **10-30 cameras**: Dedicated PC (32GB RAM, modern CPU, SSD storage)
- **30-100+ cameras**: Server-grade hardware or distributed deployment

The software automatically adapts to available hardware, making scaling seamless.
