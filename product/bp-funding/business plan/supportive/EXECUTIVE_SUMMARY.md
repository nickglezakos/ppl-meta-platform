## Company Overview

EyeNet Vision is an intelligent vision technology platform delivering six specialized applications across security, marketing, compliance, and operations verticals. Built on the PPL Meta platform, EyeNet combines proprietary computer vision, demographic analysis, and event-driven automation to transform passive surveillance into active, responsive operational intelligence.

**Core Value Proposition:** Privacy-first, on-premises vision analytics with real-time automated actions—no cloud dependency, full offline capability, infinitely scalable from single camera to enterprise-wide deployment.

## Product Portfolio

### Six Specialized Applications (5 Ready Q2 2026, 1 in Q3/Q4 2026)

1. **📺 Intelligent Signage** - Demographic-driven content delivery for retail marketing
2. **🚪 Gate Activity** - Security monitoring with crowd analytics and threat detection
3. **🔐 Room & Gate Protection** - Attendance tracking and access control with compliance reporting
4. **👮 Sentinel** - Automated security personnel monitoring for guard accountability
5. **🔞 Underage Detector** - Point-of-sale age verification for regulatory compliance
6. **👓 Security Officer Agent** - Wearable technology for mobile field operations (Q3/Q4 2026)

### Deployment Flexibility
Five deployment modes adapt to diverse environments: Spot (POS/displays), Gate (entrances/corridors), Area (large spaces), Goggles (wearable), Drone (aerial surveillance).

## Risk Mitigation

### Key Risks & Mitigation Strategies

**Regulatory/Privacy Risk**
- Modular design allows disabling demographic features by jurisdiction
- On-premises deployment reduces data sovereignty concerns
- Age-only detection (Underage Detector) minimizes PII collection

**Market Competition**
- Vertical specialization creates defensible positions
- Six applications spread risk across customer bases
- Proprietary algorithms difficult to replicate

**Technology Execution**
- Software-first approach reduces hardware dependencies
- 5 of 6 products ready Q2 2026
- Hardware partnerships for Security Officer Agent

**Market Adoption**
- Conservative penetration assumptions (0.5-3%)
- Diversified customer base across industries
- Dual B2B/B2C model reduces concentration risk

**Economic Downturn**
- Compliance products (Room & Gate Protection, Underage Detector) are non-discretionary
- Security spending remains stable in downturns
- Low hardware requirements reduce customer capex barrier
