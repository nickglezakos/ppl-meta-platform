# EyeNet Vision Financial Projections
## Dual-Model Revenue Strategy: Enterprise B2B + Consumer B2C

**Planning Period**: 2026-2028  
**Model**: Freemium B2C + Enterprise B2B SaaS  
**Update Date**: February 15, 2026  

---

## Executive Summary

Dual revenue model combining enterprise B2B subscriptions with consumer-focused B2C offerings creates diversified revenue streams and market penetration across market segments:

- **B2B Enterprise**: Existing strategy targeting large organizations, security firms, retailers ($5K-$25K+ annual contracts)
- **B2C Light/Consumer**: New entry addressing independent professionals, small businesses, and prosumers ($99-$299/month)

### **NEW: B2C Light Version (Freemium + Self-Serve)**

#### 1. **Intelligent Signage Lite** 
**Target**: Marketing consultants, freelance marketers, small agencies, retail entrepreneurs  
**Model**: Freemium → Premium  
- **Free Tier**: 1 camera, 1 display, limited analytics (60-day trial)
- **Lite Tier**: $29.99/month - 2 cameras, basic demographics, standard reporting
- **Pro Tier**: $99.99/month - 10 cameras, advanced analytics, content scheduling
- **Deployment**: Single location, pop-up events, small retail

#### 2. **Home Security Lite** (Derived from Sentinel + Gate Activity)
**Target**: Homeowners, small property managers, small security consultants  
**Model**: Freemium → Premium  
- **Free Tier**: 1 camera, motion alerts, 7-day cloud storage
- **Lite Tier**: $19.99/month - Up to 4 cameras, person detection, 30-day storage
- **Pro Tier**: $49.99/month - Unlimited cameras, facial recognition, person-of-interest alerts, 90-day storage
- **Deployment**: Home, small property, vacation rental monitoring

#### 3. **Event Security Lite** (Derived from Sentinel)
**Target**: Independent security contractors, small event security teams, venue managers  
**Model**: Per-event SaaS  
- **Basic Event**: $199/event - 1-2 camera setup, real-time monitoring, 24-hour support
- **Premium Event**: $499/event - 4-6 cameras, multiple operator access, threat alerts
- **Enterprise Event**: $1,200+/event - Unlimited cameras, advanced analytics, dedicated coordinator
- **Deployment**: Concerts, conferences, conventions, sports events

#### 4. **Retail Compliance Lite** (Derived from Underage Detector)
**Target**: Small retail owners, independent bar/restaurant owners, convenience store operators  
**Model**: Per-location SaaS  
- **Basic**: $49.99/month - Single POS counter, age detection logs, basic compliance reports
- **Pro**: $99.99/month - Multiple POS counters, detailed compliance documentation, audit trail
- **Deployment**: Single location shops, small retail chains (2-5 locations)

## Key Assumptions & Risks

### Growth Assumptions
- B2B enterprise maintains 3X year-over-year growth (market penetration)
- B2C achieves 5X growth through viral/word-of-mouth mechanisms
- Security Officer Agent ramps to 100 units by 2028 due to wearable hardware availability
- No major competitive disruption from incumbent security firms

### Risks & Mitigation
- **Regulatory/Privacy**: B2C compliance more stringent → implement privacy-first architecture
- **Market Competition**: Larger competitors enter → focus on vertical specialization and customer lock-in
- **Hardware Constraints** (Security Officer Agent): Delayed smart glasses → pivot to Goggles-only Mode initially
- **Churn Risk** (B2C): Implement engagement features, community, freemium-to-paid conversion optimization
- **Market Adoption**: Conservative 1-3% penetration targets built into projections
