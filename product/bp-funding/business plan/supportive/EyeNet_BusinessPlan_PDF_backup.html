Business Model

EyeNet operates a dual revenue model combining B2B enterprise contracts and B2C consumer subscriptions, providing stability and volume growth opportunities.

B2B Enterprise Model

  • Target Customers: Mid to large enterprises (50+ employees)
  • Contract Value: $6,000 - $150,000 annually
  • Sales Cycle: 6-9 months
  • Revenue 2026-2028: $600K → $4.5M → $14.5M

B2C Consumer Model

  • Target Customers: Small businesses, individual users
  • Pricing: $20-$300 per month

Risk Factors & Mitigation

1. Regulatory & Privacy Compliance

Risk: GDPR, CCPA, facial recognition bans limit market opportunity

Mitigation: Modular design allows disabling demographic features; age-only detection reduces PII collection; on-premises deployment option

2. Market Competition

Risk: Large players (Google, Amazon, Meta) enter with massive resources

Mitigation: Vertical specialization (6 focused applications vs. horizontal platform); proprietary algorithms; strong customer lock-in

3. Customer Concentration

Risk: Early-stage dependency on pilot customers

Mitigation: Diversified customer base across verticals; B2B + B2C dual model; no customer >15% of revenue policy
